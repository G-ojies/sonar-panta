# User testing: outreach and questions

Goal: 5 to 10 named testers before the 6 October submission, each with one quotable line. Testing costs nothing: sandbox mode runs the whole flow with an empty wallet.

## 1. Group message (Superteam Nigeria builder Telegram)

```
Hi all, Great here, building Sonar for the World's Fair.

Sonar reads the trades on every Panta prediction market and tells you which side the flow favours, with reasons. I replayed it over 78 resolved markets: it made 44 calls and 25 were right. The same engine has 672 scored signals on Kalshi and Polymarket at 57.7%.

I need 5 people to try it for 10 minutes and tell me what is confusing. No money needed, there is a sandbox mode that works with an empty wallet.

https://sonarpanta.xyz

If you trade, or you have ever wanted to create a market on the naira, CBN or the Super Eagles, I would value your view most. Happy to test your project in return. Reply here or DM me.
```

## 2. Direct message (someone who trades or has created a market)

```
Hi [name], I saw [your post on X / your market on Panta / your message in the group]. I am building Sonar, a tool that reads the trades on Panta prediction markets and says which side the flow favours.

Could you spare 10 minutes to try it and tell me where it lost you? No money or sign-up needed.

https://sonarpanta.xyz
```

## 4. What to ask them to do

Send these after they agree. Three tasks, in this order:

1. Open the Radar and pick the market you find most interesting. Tell me what Sonar thinks will happen and why.
2. Switch to Sandbox (top of the page) and place a practice trade on that market.
3. Open Create, load one of the Nigeria board questions, and read the rule. Would you trust this market to settle fairly?

## 5. Questions to ask afterwards

1. In your own words, what does Sonar do?
2. Where did you get stuck or confused?
3. Did the reasons behind a signal make sense to you? Would you act on one?
4. Which Nigerian question would you most want a market on?
5. May I quote you by name or handle in my hackathon submission?

Question 1 tests whether the product explains itself. If testers cannot answer it, fix the first screen before anything else.

## 7. Rules for using the results

- Quote only people who said yes to question 5.
- Report the number honestly: "7 testers, 4 completed a sandbox trade" is stronger than a vague "users love it".
- Write down what confused people even when it is unflattering. Fixing it and saying so is a better story for judges than a clean sheet.
