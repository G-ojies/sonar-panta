# Brand — Sonar for Panta

_Status: active_

**Voice:** terse, trader-grade, factual. Numbers over adjectives. No hype words.

**Palette (deep navy, rounded, two-voice accent), 2026-09-25:**
- Ink `#0b0c14` (bg) · Ink-2 `#13151f` (cards) · Ink-3 `#1b1e2b` (raised, active)
- Line `#262a3a` (hairlines)
- Paper `#f2f4f8` (primary text) · Fog `#9aa3b8` (secondary) · Fog-2 `#6a7288` (muted)
- Ping `#5b8def` electric blue (brand accent: links, YES line, focus) · Violet `#a78bfa` (NO line, second brand voice); the two form the logo and primary-button gradient
- YES `#34d399` · NO `#fb7185` · Amber `#fbbf24` (warnings, stale data)
- Radius: cards 16px, controls 12px, chips and filters full pill
- Logo: three white arcs fanning from a source dot on a blue-to-violet tile (`src/components/Logo.tsx`, `src/app/icon.svg`)

**Typography:** Inter (UI) + JetBrains Mono (numbers, addresses, tape). Numbers always tabular.

**Motion:** 150–200ms ease-out for state changes; the 4s radar sweep and the 60s bottom ticker are the only continuous motion, both off under reduced motion.

**Attribution:** every Panta-powered surface carries "Powered by Panta" linking to https://panta.market.
