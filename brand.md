# Brand — Sonar for Panta

_Status: active_

**Voice:** terse, trader-grade, factual. Numbers over adjectives. No hype words.

**Palette (dark-first terminal):**
- Ink `#0b0f14` (bg) · Ink-2 `#111821` (panels) · Ink-3 `#182230` (raised)
- Line `#243140` (borders)
- Paper `#e8eef5` (primary text) · Fog `#9fb0c3` (secondary) · Fog-2 `#6b7d91` (muted)
- Ping `#37d7c4` (brand accent: sonar ping / links / focus) · Ping-dim `#1d7f75`
- YES `#4ade80` · NO `#f87171` · Amber `#fbbf24` (warnings, stale data)

**Typography:** Inter (UI) + JetBrains Mono (numbers, addresses, tape). Numbers always tabular.

**Motion:** 150–200ms ease-out for state changes; a slow 2.4s "ping" ring for live indicators only. Respect reduced motion.

**Attribution:** every Panta-powered surface carries "Powered by Panta" linking to https://panta.market.
